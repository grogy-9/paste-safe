How it works

Three steps, about ten seconds.

No install and no upload. The checking is done by JavaScript running in this page, on your own device.

1

Paste your draft

Drop in the email, client note or message you were about to give an AI.

2

See what's flagged

Paste Safe highlights anything that looks like an email, phone number, IBAN, PPS number, password or address.

3

Copy the scrubbed version

Flags become placeholders like [EMAIL] and [PHONE]. Read it over yourself, then paste.

Emails → [EMAIL] Irish mobiles / +353 → [PHONE] IBAN-like → [IBAN] PPS-like → [PPS_OR_ID] Long digit runs → [NUMBER] Password cues → [PASSWORD_REMOVED] Address cues → [ADDRESS_DETAIL]

Who it's for

People who handle other people's details.

It's built with solo workers and small operators in Ireland in mind. That's a starting guess, not something proven yet.

Probably a good fit

  • Freelancers and sole traders who ask AI to tidy up client emails
  • Tutors, coaches and consultants working from session notes
  • Small shops and makers answering customer messages
  • Anyone who has pasted something into AI and then wondered whether they should have

Not a fit

  • Teams that need enterprise data-loss prevention
  • Anyone who needs a legal, GDPR or DPC opinion
  • Highly sensitive data such as health records or legal files, where you need proper processes

Honest limits

What Paste Safe is not.

  • It's pattern matching. It looks for shapes of text, such as something@something or 08x numbers. It will miss things and sometimes flag harmless text.
  • It is not a legal or GDPR tool. Using it doesn't make anything "compliant" and it isn't legal advice.
  • It doesn't find names. "Nora from Ranelagh" gets through. You still need to check the text yourself.
  • PPS and IBAN checks are format guesses. It doesn't validate them.
  • It's new. This is version 0.1 of an experiment. There are no users, reviews or results to show you yet, so none are claimed.

FAQ

Questions

Does my text get uploaded anywhere?

Not by this page. The scan runs as JavaScript in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your text. The code is public on GitHub so anyone can check. Browser extensions you've installed are outside the page's control.

Is it really free?

Yes. The tool is free and open source under the MIT licence. You need no account and it shows no ads.

Does this make me GDPR compliant?

No. It's a convenience tool that catches common patterns. For data protection obligations, see the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie) or get proper advice.

Which AI tools does it work with?

All of them, because it works on your text before it gets there. You copy the scrubbed version and paste it wherever you like. It isn't affiliated with OpenAI, Google, Microsoft or any AI company.

Can I buy the monthly drop?

Not yet. It's an idea being tested. The button only opens an enquiry email, so nothing is sold or charged on this page.
